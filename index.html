THE MUSAFIR CHRONICLES

Built for the Dreamer, the Roamer & the Coffee Lover

In Persian and Hindi, "Musafir" means the traveler—someone whose heart finds home not in a fixed destination, but in the journeys taken, the conversations shared, and the quiet moments between mountains.

We chose Jharipani—the quieter, greener foothill ridge of Mussoorie—to create a sanctuary where the scent of roasted coffee beans mingles with pine resin and woodsmoke.
